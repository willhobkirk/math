My research focus is in Algebraic and Geometric Topology.

Lens spaces are a family of 3-manifolds obtained by identifying the boundaries of two solid tori. The Lens space $L(k,q)$ where $\gcd(k,q)= 1$ is given by identifying the solid tori such that the meridional curve of one solid torus is identified with a $(k,q)$-torus knot on the boundary of the other solid torus.

$S^3 = L(1,0)$

$S^1 \times S^2 = L(0,1)$

The real projective space $\mathbb{R}\text{P}^3 = L(2,1)$

The Lens space $L(7,2)$

The Lens spaces $L(2n,q)$ have a non-trivial $H_2(\mathbb{Z}_2)$ which is realised geometrically by a non-orientable surface $X$ embedded in $L(2n,1)$ which does not separate the embedding manifold (equivalently $X$ is connected). The maximum Euler characteristic of such a non-orientable surface embedded in $L(2n,1)$ is $2-n$ by a result of Bredon and Wood. What is the correct object to realise the non-trivial $H_2(\mathbb{Z}_p)$ classes of the Lens spaces $L(pn,q)$? The answer: $p$-folded surfaces. A $p$-Folded surface is an oriented folded surface in the sense of Turaev where the orientation of the interior induces a consistent orientation along each component of the singular locus and the index of every component of the singular locus is p.
